4. Missions and physical activity

FirstBoss missions ask you to move — most of them ask you to throw punches at things only your camera can see. You will usually be doing this in a bedroom, in the dark, seconds after waking up, which is exactly when people misjudge where the wall is.

Before you play:

  • Stand or sit where you have a clear arm's length in every direction. Watch for walls, headboards, nightstands, lamps, ceiling fans, light fixtures, mirrors, windows, and TVs.
  • Make sure no one else is in range — including partners, children, and pets.
  • Don't play on or from a top bunk, a loft bed, a balcony, stairs, or any raised surface you could fall from.
  • Take off rings, watches, and anything else on your hands.
  • Don't play while holding anything you don't want to throw.

Never play a mission while driving, cycling, walking, or doing anything else that needs your attention. Stop immediately if you feel pain, dizziness, numbness, or discomfort, and talk to a doctor before playing if you have any condition that movement or flashing visuals could affect.
