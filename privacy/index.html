FirstBoss Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 10, 2026

FirstBoss (the "App") is made by Limitless Labs LLC ("we," "us," "our"). This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

The short version

  • Your camera never leaves your phone. Gesture missions are processed on your device and nothing is recorded or uploaded.
  • No ads. We don't sell your data, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.
  • You can delete everything the App has stored from inside the App at any time.

Some things described below — accounts, cloud sync, and the tip jar — arrive in later versions of the App. We describe them here so this policy stays accurate as they roll out, and so you can see in advance exactly what each one will and won't collect.

1. Information we collect

Account information. FirstBoss does not require an account to set an alarm or play a mission. When sign-in becomes available, you'll be able to create an account with Sign in with Apple or an email address. If you use Sign in with Apple, we receive the identifier Apple provides, which may be a private relay email address. We never see your Apple ID password.

App data. Your alarm settings, mission progress, streak, and wake-up stats. This is stored on your device, in the App's own private storage. If you sign in, it is also synced to our servers so it's available across your devices and survives a new phone.

Usage and diagnostic data. Information about how you use the App, such as screens viewed, alarms dismissed, missions completed, and crashes, along with device model, iOS version, app version, language, and approximate region. We use this to understand what's working and fix what isn't. Our analytics provider is configured to discard IP addresses, so we don't build a location history from them.

Purchase information. If you leave a tip, we receive a record of the purchase (product, date, price, and an anonymous app user ID) so we can confirm it. Apple processes all payments. We never see or store your payment card details.

Support messages. If you contact us, we keep your email address and what you send us so we can respond.

2. Camera and gesture missions

Some missions use your camera to detect gestures or movement. When you play one:

  • The camera is active only while a mission you started is on screen.
  • Video frames are analyzed in real time, on your device, using Apple's Vision framework.
  • Frames are never recorded, saved, uploaded to our servers, or shared with anyone.
  • We do not use the camera to identify you, and we do not create or keep face templates or any other biometric identifiers.
  • In a dark room, the App may switch to the rear camera and turn on the torch so it can still see your hands. The same rules apply: analyzed live, never recorded.

You can deny or revoke camera access anytime in iOS Settings. Camera-based missions won't be available, but the rest of the App will still work.

3. Microphone and voice missions

Some missions can be played with your voice instead of your hands. When one is running, audio is analyzed on your device, in memory, for loudness and pitch only — enough to tell a shout from a whisper. Nothing is recorded, written to disk, uploaded, or transcribed, and we do not use audio to identify you or recognize speech.

You can deny or revoke microphone access anytime in iOS Settings. Voice missions won't be available; nothing else changes.

4. Device permissions

  • Alarms and notifications: so your alarms can ring, including when the App is closed.
  • Camera: for gesture missions only, as described above.
  • Microphone: for voice missions only, as described above.

We only ask for a permission when you use the feature that needs it.

5. How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • Run your alarms and missions, and keep your progress
  • Create and maintain your account
  • Confirm tip purchases
  • Understand usage, fix bugs, and improve the App
  • Respond to support requests
  • Comply with legal obligations and protect the App from abuse

We do not sell your personal information, share it for targeted advertising, or use it to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

7. How long we keep it

  • Account and app data: for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete this data within 30 days.
  • Analytics data: up to 12 months, then deleted or aggregated so it no longer identifies you.
  • Purchase records: as long as required for tax, accounting, and legal purposes.
  • Support messages: up to 24 months after your request is resolved.

Anything stored only on your device is gone the moment you delete it in the App, or delete the App.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your data: In the App, tap the gear on the Home screen, then Account → Delete account & data. This erases your alarms, streak, and stats on your phone, and there is no undo. If you've signed in, it also deletes your account and any synced data from our servers within 30 days.
  • Camera and microphone: Revoke access anytime in iOS Settings.
  • Access or correct your data: Email us at support@ottowrites.co.

Depending on where you live (for example, the EU, UK, or California), you may have additional rights, such as to access, correct, delete, or port your data, object to or restrict processing, or withdraw consent. Email us to exercise them. We'll respond within the time required by law and won't discriminate against you for doing so. If you're in the EU or UK, you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Where the GDPR applies, we rely on: performance of a contract (running the App and your account), legitimate interests (analytics, security, and improving the App), consent (camera and microphone access), and legal obligation (records we must keep).

9. Children

FirstBoss is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where local law sets a higher age). We don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

10. Security

We use reasonable safeguards, including encryption in transit and access controls, to protect your information. No system is perfectly secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security.

11. International users

We're based in the United States, and our service providers may process data in the U.S. and other countries. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for international transfers.

12. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we'll update the effective date above and let you know in the App. Continuing to use FirstBoss after changes take effect means you accept the updated policy.
